01 — A little about me

From structures
to systems.

My work has grown from civil engineering on the ground to understanding how people, places and risk connect.

I’m a civil engineer with a master’s degree in Disaster Risk Management. In Nepal, I’ve worked across reconstruction, risk mapping, field data, multi-hazard early warning and local disaster risk governance.

I bring engineering practice and spatial analysis together with careful coordination—helping teams turn field observations and data into information that communities and decision-makers can use.

07 — How I think about the work

Good risk information should travel beyond the map.

Engineering taught me to pay attention to how things are built. Disaster risk work has taught me to look just as closely at who is exposed, what information is missing, and how decisions reach the people they affect.

That is why I value clear analysis, reliable field data and collaboration with the institutions and communities closest to the work.
